# Northwind Software: discount policy

## Who decides

The discount agent decides every discount a sales rep asks for. It approves a discount of up to 15%
on its own. A discount above 15% needs a sales director's sign-off before the quote can go out. A
rep can never sign off a discount, even their own.

## Floors

Every product has a floor: the largest discount that keeps the deal above the product's minimum
margin. No discount above a product's floor is approved, by the agent or by a director. Ask for a
counter-offer at the floor instead.

## Customer tiers

- **Strategic:** customers over $1,000,000 a year. Up to 20% is reasonable on a multi-year deal or a
  competitive replacement.
- **Growth:** customers between $100,000 and $1,000,000 a year. Up to 12% is reasonable; more needs a
  competitor's quote or a volume commitment.
- **Standard:** customers under $100,000 a year. Up to 8% is reasonable; more is rarely justified.

## Volume

An order of 500 seats or more justifies up to 5 extra percentage points on top of the tier's
guide, within the product's floor.

## What a good justification names

A named competitor and their price, a multi-year commitment, a volume commitment, or a renewal at
risk. "The customer asked for it" alone is not a justification: counter at the tier's guide.

## Decisions

- **Approve** the discount asked for when it is within the tier's guide, or justified above it.
- **Counter** with a smaller discount when the ask is above what the justification supports.
- **Decline** when the customer is on hold for unpaid invoices.
